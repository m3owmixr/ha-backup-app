HA-Backup Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What this app is

HA-Backup is a personal OAuth client used by one person, its owner, to connect their own Home Assistant server to their own Google Drive for storing backups.

What data it accesses

With your permission, the app uploads, lists, downloads and deletes backup files in a Home Assistant folder it creates in your Google Drive. It can only reach files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. It also reads storage usage figures so Home Assistant can show how much space is left.

Where data goes

Backups travel straight from your Home Assistant server to your Google Drive. Home Assistant encrypts them before upload. The Google sign-in token is kept only on your own Home Assistant server. No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with the app's owner or anyone else. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HA-Backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the Google Drive integration in Home Assistant. Backups already in Drive stay there until you delete them.
